# What I'd redo in Pulse

Reflections from building Pulse (2024–2026). Every figure and quoted line is a **placeholder** — replace with verified facts before publishing.

## High-level takeaways

- The feed architecture that works well in a monorepo may not scale the same way in a solo project.
- Early investment in developer tooling (type-safe APIs, CI checks) pays off more than UI polish.
- Moving moderation to the edge (Cloudflare Workers) reduced origin load and improved latency.

## What to revisit

1. **Feed pagination** — cursor-based pagination works, but a simpler chronological list might be more robust for early users.
2. **Moderation pipeline** — the rule engine was runtime; an edge-first approach is cleaner.
3. **Onboarding flow** — the first-run trophy and guided tour proved effective for retention.
