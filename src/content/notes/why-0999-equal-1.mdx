# Why 0.999… = 1

A classic question in real analysis.

Consider the geometric series:

$$
0.999\ldots = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{9}{10^n} = \frac{9/10}{1 - 1/10} = \frac{9/10}{9/10} = 1
$$

The infinite sum converges to 1. In the standard real number system, there is no number between 0.999… and 1, so they are equal.
